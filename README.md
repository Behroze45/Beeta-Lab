# Beeta Lab

## Overview

Beeta Lab is an AI-powered Historical Intelligence Platform designed for creators and researchers. Rather than functioning as a traditional chatbot, it proactively helps users discover upcoming historical anniversaries, connect related events and people, verify information, and prepare high-quality, citation-backed content before important dates.

The AI acts as an intelligent copilot integrated into the workspace, enabling research, content planning, and historical exploration.
